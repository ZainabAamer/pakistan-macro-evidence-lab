Would five crisis cases fool your risk model?

Five carefully chosen policy days made USD/PKR moves look exceptional. We widened the test to every listed SBP policy statement in five years, then checked a later year separately. The difference shrank sharply. Explore the calculation and the cases behind it.

The research result: five selected crisis cases averaged a 7.80% maximum 20-observation FX reference move, versus 5.14% across all 17 2022–2023 policy statements. The small 2025 difference cautions against a general policy-day risk claim. This is observational and uses nontradable reference prices.

Does the horizon change the answer?

Differences are percentage points: mean policy-date maximum move minus same-year mean control move. The exact two-sided sign-flip reference swaps signs of the five annual differences rather than treating overlapping daily windows as independent. Exchangeable year-level signs are an unverified assumption; 2025 alone cannot support a significance test.

All three 2025 estimates are small. The 20-observation five-year reference p is 0.0625; with only five paired years, this does not prove the effect is zero or economically negligible. The shorter horizons were inspected after the 20-observation result: they are exploratory robustness checks.

Evidence checking · measured pilot

Where the NLP baseline failed

The TF-IDF fact retriever plus numeric/polarity checks scored 25 of 30 related claims correctly and abstained three times. It wrongly accepted an August inflation claim of 38% because 38% appeared as a May peak in the same September 2023 statement: matching a number cannot establish its date.

These claims are derived from 15 manually checked facts in five documents. They are not 30 independent examples or an LLM evaluation. A runner for an external local LLM is included; no LLM output is asserted.

What an investor can conclude

Source discipline beats a neat signal

Different horizons can reverse a conclusion. After 7 April 2022, the 20-observation endpoint was −0.79%; by the eve of the next policy meeting the change was +6.97%. The five-case result was selected with hindsight; the broader sample provides a check on that choice.

The script, source-linked dates, 3,170 historical reference observations, controls and failure logs are included in the project archive. The current work does not demonstrate a tradable FX edge or measure benefit to real users.
